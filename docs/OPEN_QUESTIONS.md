# Open Questions

Direction questions whose answers reshape the roadmap. Each question says **what it changes**. The owner answers here, in chat, or both; the agent records every answer in the table at the end. **Never ask an answered question again.**

Kickoff seeds this file from the interview. Add a question whenever a choice would change what the product *is* (smaller choices are decided within the design and logged in `STATE.md`).

## Template

**Q<n>. <The question, in one sentence>?**
<Two or three lines of context: the options, and what each one changes in the roadmap.>

---

## Direction

**Q1. What is the main end product for the next few months?**
{{options}}. This decides which milestone comes first.

**Q2. Who else will use it, and when?**
Only the owner; a collaborator soon; the public later. This decides when hosting, login and permissions matter.

**Q3. What monthly budget for paid APIs and services?**
For example under $20, $20–100, or more. This sets the default models, the paid-call gate and the monthly cap.

**Q4. How much should agents do on their own?**
For example a nightly agent that prepares a round of proposals but never publishes. Welcome, or noise?

**Q5. How often will the owner work on this?**
In bursts or steadily. This decides whether milestones carry dates.
